About us

We’re two girls from Turin who love workshops: ceramics, embroidery, cooking, printing and much more.

Every time we wanted to try something new we ended up scrolling through dozens of Instagram profiles, posters and websites, and we often discovered the best workshops when they were already over. So we decided to put everything in one place.

The Crafty Club gathers workshops, courses and activities in Turin, Cuneo and around, always up to date and with everything you need to know: when, where and how much it costs.

Why we do it

  • For anyone who wants to learn something new, meet people or treat themselves to a different afternoon.
  • For small makers, studios and local associations, who do wonderful things but don’t always manage to be found.
  • Because we believe a city full of things to learn and try is a more alive city.
