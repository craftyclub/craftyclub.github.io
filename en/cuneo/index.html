Workshops, courses and activities in Turin and Cuneo

The Crafty Club gathers in one place the workshops, courses and activities coming up in Piedmont. You’ll find ceramics and wheel throwing, cooking classes, sewing, knitting and embroidery, painting and watercolour, wine tastings, dance, music and lots of activities for kids and families.

For every event you’ll find the date, time, place, price, organizer and the link to sign up. The site is free and always up to date.

Event details come from the organizers: always check with them before you go. Names, brands and images belong to their owners.
